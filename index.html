Is HerVerse jewellery anti-tarnish?

Our pieces are designed with anti-tarnish properties. Actual longevity depends on wear and care, so please follow the care instructions supplied with your order.

How should I care for my jewellery?

Keep jewellery away from water, perfume, lotions and harsh chemicals. Store it dry in an airtight pouch or box and wipe gently after wearing.

Do you ship across India?

Yes. HerVerse ships across India. Delivery times and charges can vary by location.
