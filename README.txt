# HerVerse Website

This is a static, mobile-friendly storefront for HerVerse.

## Before publishing
Open `script.js` and replace:
1. `WHATSAPP_NUMBER` with your real WhatsApp number in international format, without + or spaces.
2. `INSTAGRAM_URL` with your real Instagram profile.

Then edit the `products` list in `script.js` to add your real products, prices, categories and names.

## Product photos
The current site uses elegant placeholder artwork so it works immediately. To use real product photos, replace each `.product-image` block in `script.js` with an `<img>` pointing to your image, or ask for a version that includes your uploaded product photos.

## Free hosting
This is plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript, so it can be hosted on many free static-hosting services. Your existing `theherverse.in` domain can then be connected through your domain provider's DNS settings.

## Important
The WhatsApp ordering flow is ready, but the WhatsApp number must be changed before launch. Payment gateway checkout is not included because a gateway account is required.
